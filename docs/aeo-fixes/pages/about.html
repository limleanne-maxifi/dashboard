About Maxifi Digital

Maxifi Digital is a Singapore-based Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) consultancy that makes expert-led brands the source AI engines cite. We work with companies whose authority is real but invisible to AI — aviation bodies, law firms, think tanks, and professional services — and build the visibility layer that gets them retrieved, understood, and quoted by name across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Copilot. The firm was founded by Lim Le-Anne.

Founder

Maxifi Digital was founded by Lim Le-Anne. She brings a senior communications background spanning Singapore's public sector and industry, where she spent years translating complex expertise into messages people trust. Working at that intersection, she observed a structural gap: AI engines cannot cite expertise that isn't structured for retrieval, so the most knowledgeable organisations were being left out of AI answers. She built Maxifi Digital to close that gap and turn genuine expertise into citable authority.

What we do

The problem is simple: AI can't cite what it can't parse. Buyers now screen vendors by asking AI engines, and brands that aren't structured for retrieval are absent from the answer — no matter how strong their reputation. Our method is the Visibility Engine, a continuous loop: a Snapshot audits your citation share across engines and buyer questions, a Sprint structures and publishes the assets engines can cite, and ongoing Monitoring tracks and compounds your share over time.

Where we work

Maxifi Digital is based in Singapore and works with clients globally. We operate from one office — Singapore — and serve organisations wherever their buyers are asking AI.
